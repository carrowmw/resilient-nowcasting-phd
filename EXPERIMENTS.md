# Experiment Ledger

This ledger records the major experimental stages, architectural milestones, and empirical outcomes across the project. For full methodology, mathematical formulations, and reproduction instructions, see [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](docs/EXPERIMENTS.md) and [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

| Date | Stage / Track | Summary of Experiment & Objective | Empirical & Functional Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2026-10-28 | **RIoT v7 Stage** (`configs/train/v7/`) | **6-Seed Confirmatory Suite (48 GPU runs, 198 GPU-h)**: Evaluate static-feature ridge profile prior (`v7_profile_static`), block masks (`v7_block`, `v7_static_block`), target vs. origin weather extremes, and 150-epoch training budgets (`v7_base_e150`, `v7_profile_static_e150`) across 6 seeds (`42–47`) on all 57 training stations and 24 held-out stations. | **Completed & Verified** ([`docs/experiments/riot_v7/riot_v7_seed_report.md`](docs/experiments/riot_v7/riot_v7_seed_report.md)). Static-ridge wins 16/18 seed-matched pairs across settings (sign test $p = 0.031$). `v7_profile_static_e150` achieves the best overall metrics: mean all-node RMSE **23.89 ± 2.18** (best seed `s47`: **20.08**), held-out RMSE **41.31 ± 4.10** (`s47`: **33.30**), transductive RMSE **11.55 ± 0.15**, and `SO@12` outage RMSE **13.88 ± 0.21**. |
| 2026-10-22 | **RIoT v6 Stage** (`configs/train/v6/`) | **Protocol Hardening & Static-Ridge Prior (78 GPU runs, 289 GPU-h)**: Introduce cosine LR schedule with 3-epoch warmup, `min_epochs=40`, and `inductive_static` ridge regression profile prior across 26 experiments × 3 seeds (`42–44`). | **Completed & Verified** ([`docs/experiments/riot_v6/riot_v6_seed_report.md`](docs/experiments/riot_v6/riot_v6_seed_report.md)). Cosine schedule halved transductive seed variance (1.0% vs 2.0%). `v6_profile_static` reduced held-out RMSE by **8.5%** (41.72 vs 45.59) and all-node RMSE by **7.1%** (24.89 vs 26.81) while maintaining `SO@12` RMSE at **14.50** and `SO@96` mass at **0.91**. |
| 2026-10-09 | **Spatial Sparsity Scaling** (`configs/train/v5_scaling/`) | **Network Density Scaling Suite (69 GPU runs)**: Sweep active training station count $N_{\text{train}} \in \{15, 25, 35, 45, 51, 57\}$ against 24 frozen held-out stations to quantify inductive GNN transfer vs. nearest geographic donor baselines. | **Completed & Verified** ([`docs/experiments/riot_v5_scaling/scaling_summary.md`](docs/experiments/riot_v5_scaling/scaling_summary.md)). Fitted power-law scaling curve $\text{RMSE}(N) = A \cdot N^{-\alpha} + \text{RMSE}_\infty$. Inductive GNN surpasses the naive geographic donor baseline at $N_{\text{train}} = 57$ ($2.71\text{ stations/km}^2$, RMSE **40.42** vs **42.63**). |
| 2026-10-08 | **RIoT v5 Suite** (`configs/train/v5/`) | **Zero-Collapse Remediation & 7-Phase Ablation Suite (209 GPU runs)**: Implement unified missingness encoding, structured masking (`iid`, `block`, `transplant`, `tail`, `mixed`), seasonal profile priors, NB/ZINB/outage-mixture likelihoods, and leave-one-out (LOO) ablations. | **Completed & Verified** ([`docs/experiments/riot_v5/riot_v5_seed_report.md`](docs/experiments/riot_v5/riot_v5_seed_report.md)). Solved sensor outage zero-collapse: 3h synthetic outage `SO@12` RMSE dropped **50%** (31.8 → 15.8), 24h outage predicted mass ratio rose from **0.00 to 1.01**, and transductive RMSE improved **23%** (17.0 → 13.1). |
| 2026-10-01 | **ERA5 Weather Pipeline** (`scripts/ingestion/`) | **Open-Meteo Historical Weather Cache**: Replace gapped local weather station readings with complete 15-minute ERA5 reanalysis (`Humidity`, `Solar Radiation`) across all 104 sensor coordinates (`2023-01-08` to `2026-06-08`). | **Completed & Verified**. Persisted 12.46M rows to `data/external/weather_cache.parquet` (54 MB) with physical bounds clamping and zero missing values. |
| 2026-09-28 | **RIoT v4 Rebuild** (`configs/train/v4/`) | **Audit Remediation & Baseline Rebuild**: Implement purged 70/15/15 chronological splits, train-only scalers, explicit MNAR masks, sinusoidal positional encodings, and GATv2 holdout isolation. | **Completed & Verified**. Closed temporal boundary and scaler leakage across all model adapters and exporters. |
| 2026-09-11 | **Track 2: Spatial GAT** (`src/spatial/`) | **Spatial Dependence Track**: Build decoupled `src/spatial/` package with paired `GraphForecaster` contract over `[B, N, T, D]` batches, comparing `identity` (matched-capacity control), `gabriel`, `knn`, `fc`, and `learned` topologies with Diebold–Mariano + BH-FDR testing. | **Completed & Verified**. 81/81 unit tests and 3 mathematical desk checks passed; full multi-seed spatial suite executed and aggregated. |
| 2026-07-22 | **Track 1: Quality Track** (`src/quality/`) | **Per-Sensor Observability & Quality Track**: Build `src/quality/` package evaluating 81 directional pedestrian streams across 6 model families and 5 interpolation regimes, with Lomb–Scargle periodograms and segmented threshold regression. | **Completed & Verified**. 48/48 unit tests and 3 desk checks passed; full 81-sensor evaluation completed. |
| 2026-06-30 | **Unified RIoT Layers 1–4** (`src/`) | **Core Multi-Task Architecture**: Implement scale-invariant raw-count MSLE imputation loss, IGNNK `SpatialKrigingDecoder`, and 10-dimensional OSM road-network edge features (`edge_dim=10`) in `DynamicGraphLearner` + `GATv2Conv`. | **Completed & Verified**. Documented in [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md). |
